TAKINARO Fighting Farm ("the game") is a cartoon fighting game for a mixed audience: children, teenagers and adults. It is published on Google Play by TAKINARO Games; its legal owner is Vladimer Gorgaslidze ("the owner").

This policy describes two different things, and keeps them apart:

  • what the game itself does with information — sections 1, 2, 4 and 5. The owner has no server, and the game's own functions send no information to the owner;
  • what the Google services built into the game process — Google AdMob and Google's consent tool, section 3. Google processes that data under its own policies; the owner does not receive it.

1. Information the game itself keeps on your device

The game saves on your device only:

  • your progress: coins, unlocked heroes and weapons, levels, campaign stars, trophies, challenges;
  • your settings: sound volumes, language, controls, graphics;
  • your age group (section 2) and the number of finished matches (it decides when the next ad is due);
  • backup copies of the save on the device: the previous save, a copy every time you use RESET DATA, and a copy whenever an update changes the save format.

There is no account and no sign-in. The game turns off Android's automatic app backup, so its files are not included in Android's cloud backup (such as Google Drive backups). On some devices Android can still copy app data during a direct device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone.

Separately, Google's consent tool (section 3) stores your consent choices on the device.

2. Your age group

Because the game shows ads, it asks for your year of birth on a neutral screen. Only the resulting age group — under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and older — is stored on your device; the year itself is not kept. The game does not send your year of birth or your age group to the owner or to Google; it uses the age group only to choose how ad requests are marked (section 3).

You can correct your answer at any time in SETTINGS → AGE. This changes only how ads are requested; it does not change your coins, unlocked items or progress. Players who answered in an older version of the game and were counted as "16 and older" are asked again, and the game shows no ads until they answer.

3. Google services built into the game: ads and consent (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform)

On Android the game shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after every second finished match, and — only if you choose it on the results screen — an ad that doubles the battle coins of that match. No ads appear during a fight, during the pause or in the local FRIENDS mode. The game works without ads when the device is offline.

Before the age question is answered, the game does not initialize Google's ads SDK or Google's consent tool and does not request any ad.

After the answer, the game uses two Google tools:

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — Google's consent tool. It asks Google whether a consent message is needed where you are and remembers your choices on the device. For players under 18 the request is marked "under the age of consent", so no consent message is shown to them.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) — it is started, and ads are requested, only when the consent tool allows ad requests.

When the ad service runs, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google, according to Google's own disclosure: the IP address (which may be used to estimate the general location of the device), product interactions (such as app launches, taps and video views), diagnostic information (app and SDK performance) and device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID, the app set ID and other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device). Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention; it is encrypted in transit. This is Google's processing, not the game's own: the owner does not receive this data. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy — how Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

How the game asks Google to treat ad requests for each age group:

  • Under 13: every ad request is tagged as child-directed (COPPA). Ads are not personalized, there is no remarketing and the publisher first-party ID is turned off. According to Google's documentation, the advertising ID is not sent with these requests, and AdMob serves only ad sources certified for Google Play's Families policy.
  • 13 to 17: every ad request is tagged "under the age of consent". Ads are not personalized, there is no remarketing, third-party ad vendors are not used and the publisher first-party ID is turned off. According to Google's documentation, the advertising ID is not sent with these requests.
  • 18 and older: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent message asks for your choices before ads are requested. If your choices cannot be collected (for example offline on the first start), the game requests no ads.
  • Everyone: ads are limited to content rated for all ages (G).

Changing your choices: where Google's consent tool says privacy choices are needed, SETTINGS → PRIVACY shows PRIVACY CHOICES, which opens Google's own privacy options form. After you change your choices, the ads the game had already loaded are discarded; new ads are requested only if your new choices allow it.

4. Playing with friends (FRIENDS) — a function of the game itself

The FRIENDS mode connects two devices on the same local network (the same Wi-Fi or one phone's hotspot); no Internet is needed. The game sends these FRIENDS messages only within the local network — not to the Internet, to the owner or to a server.

  • Finding a room: while a room waits for a friend, the hosting device sends a short message about once a second to the local network's broadcast address (UDP port 24681). Every device on that local network can receive it. It contains only the game's network protocol number, the game version and a room name such as "FARM-1234" — a random number made each time the game starts, never the phone's name or model.
  • Joining: the two devices connect directly (UDP port 24680). The joining device sends the network protocol number, the game version, a checksum of the game's content (both must have the same game, else joining is refused), its random name ("FARM-…") and the list of weapons in its weapon set. Each device sees the other's local network (IP) address; the hosting device also shows its address on screen so it can be typed in.
  • In the room and the match: both random names, both weapon sets, the chosen heroes, ready states, rules and arena; then the match settings (a match number made from the time and a random number, a random seed, whether farm surprises are on), the players' button presses, the state of the fight (positions, health, effects) and timing messages to measure the connection.
  • Nothing about the other device is saved after the session. No ads are shown in FRIENDS.

5. What the game's own functions do not do

The game's own functions do not ask for or collect names, e-mail addresses, contacts, photos, precise location or payment data, and the owner does not sell personal information. The game has no chat, no account, no analytics service of its own and no in-app purchases (coins are earned only by playing). What the Google services built into the game process is described in section 3.

6. Deleting data

Everything stored for the game on your device: uninstall the game, or use the device's settings (Settings → Apps → TAKINARO Fighting Farm → Storage → Clear storage). This removes all saves, the backup copies, the age group, the settings and the consent choices stored for the game. The owner has no copy of them.

SETTINGS → RESET DATA is not a full deletion: it can lock heroes and weapons again or start a new profile, but it keeps a copy of the old save on the device (so a mistake can be undone). A new profile also clears the age group, so the age question is asked again.

Data Google received through its services in the game: this data is held by Google, not by the owner, and the owner can neither see nor delete it. Use Google's controls: https://myadcenter.google.com, Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy), and the device's ad settings (for example Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), where the advertising ID can be reset or deleted.

7. Children

The game has no account, no chat and no fields where a child could enter a name or contact details, and the game does not send such information to the owner. However, the Google services built into the game can process data according to the rules and the age protection settings described in section 3 (child-directed requests for players under 13, requests marked "under the age of consent" for players aged 13 to 17). Parents with questions can write to dinamot1981@gmail.com.

TAKINARO Fighting Farm ("el juego") es un juego de peleas de dibujos animados para un público mixto: niños, adolescentes y adultos. Lo publica en Google Play TAKINARO Games; su titular legal es Vladimer Gorgaslidze ("el titular").

El juego no envía tu año de nacimiento ni tu grupo de edad al titular ni a Google; usa el grupo de edad solo para elegir cómo se marcan las solicitudes de anuncios (sección 3).

Puedes corregir tu respuesta en cualquier momento en AJUSTES → EDAD. Esto solo cambia cómo se piden los anuncios; no cambia tus monedas, objetos desbloqueados ni tu progreso. A quienes respondieron en una versión anterior del juego y quedaron como "16 o más" se les pregunta de nuevo, y el juego no muestra anuncios hasta que respondan.

3. Servicios de Google integrados en el juego: anuncios y consentimiento (Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform)

En Android el juego muestra anuncios de Google AdMob: un anuncio corto cada dos partidas terminadas y, solo si tú lo eliges en la pantalla de resultados, un anuncio que duplica las monedas de combate de esa partida. No aparecen anuncios durante un combate, en la pausa ni en el modo local AMIGOS. Sin conexión, el juego funciona sin anuncios.

Antes de responder la pregunta de la edad, el juego no inicializa el SDK de anuncios de Google ni la herramienta de consentimiento de Google y no pide ningún anuncio.

Google usa estos datos para publicidad, análisis y prevención del fraude; se cifran en tránsito. Es un tratamiento de Google, no del propio juego: el titular no recibe estos datos. Política de privacidad de Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy — cómo usa Google la información de las apps de sus socios: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Cómo pide el juego a Google que trate las solicitudes de anuncios de cada grupo de edad:

Según la documentación de Google, no se envía el ID de publicidad con estas solicitudes, y AdMob solo usa fuentes de anuncios certificadas para la política de Familias de Google Play.

4. Jugar con amigos (AMIGOS) — una función del propio juego

El modo AMIGOS conecta dos dispositivos de la misma red local (el mismo Wi-Fi o el punto de acceso de un teléfono); no hace falta Internet. El juego envía estos mensajes de AMIGOS solo dentro de la red local — no a Internet, al titular ni a un servidor.

  • Buscar una sala: mientras una sala espera a un amigo, el dispositivo anfitrión envía un mensaje corto aproximadamente una vez por segundo a la dirección de difusión de la red local (puerto UDP 24681). Cualquier dispositivo de esa red local puede recibirlo. Contiene solo el número de protocolo de red del juego, la versión del juego y un nombre de sala como "FARM-1234", un número al azar creado cada vez que se inicia el juego, nunca el nombre ni el modelo del teléfono.
  • Unirse: los dos dispositivos se conectan directamente (puerto UDP 24680). El dispositivo que se une envía el número de protocolo de red, la versión del juego, una suma de comprobación del contenido del juego (los dos deben tener el mismo juego; si no, se rechaza la unión), su nombre al azar ("FARM-…") y la lista de armas de su juego de armas. Cada dispositivo ve la dirección de red local (IP) del otro; el anfitrión además muestra su dirección en pantalla para poder escribirla.
  • En la sala y en la partida: los dos nombres al azar, los dos juegos de armas, los héroes elegidos, quién está listo, las reglas y la arena; después, los ajustes de la partida (un número de partida hecho con la hora y un número al azar, una semilla aleatoria, si las sorpresas de la granja están activadas), los botones que pulsan los jugadores, el estado del combate (posiciones, salud, efectos) y mensajes de tiempo para medir la conexión.
  • No se guarda nada del otro dispositivo después de la sesión. En AMIGOS no hay anuncios.

El juego no tiene chat, ni cuenta, ni un servicio de análisis propio, ni compras dentro de la app (las monedas solo se ganan jugando). Lo que tratan los servicios de Google integrados en el juego se describe en la sección 3.

6. Borrar datos

Todo lo guardado para el juego en tu dispositivo: desinstala el juego o usa los ajustes del dispositivo (Ajustes → Aplicaciones → TAKINARO Fighting Farm → Almacenamiento → Borrar almacenamiento). Así se borran del dispositivo todas las partidas, las copias de seguridad, el grupo de edad, los ajustes y las opciones de consentimiento guardadas para el juego. El titular no tiene ninguna copia.

AJUSTES → REINICIAR DATOS no es un borrado completo: puede volver a bloquear héroes y armas o empezar un perfil nuevo, pero guarda una copia de la partida anterior en el dispositivo (para poder deshacer un error). Un perfil nuevo también borra el grupo de edad, así que la pregunta de la edad se hace de nuevo.

Datos que Google recibió a través de sus servicios en el juego: esos datos los tiene Google, no el titular, que no puede verlos ni borrarlos. Usa los controles de Google: https://myadcenter.google.com, la política de privacidad de Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy) y los ajustes de anuncios del dispositivo (por ejemplo Ajustes → Google → Anuncios, o Ajustes → Privacidad → Anuncios), donde puedes restablecer o eliminar el ID de publicidad.

El juego no tiene cuenta, ni chat, ni campos donde un niño pueda escribir su nombre o sus datos de contacto, y el juego no envía esa información al titular. Sin embargo, los servicios de Google integrados en el juego pueden tratar datos según las reglas y los ajustes de protección por edad descritos en la sección 3 (solicitudes dirigidas a niños para los menores de 13 años, solicitudes marcadas como "menor de la edad de consentimiento" para quienes tienen de 13 a 17 años). Los padres con preguntas pueden escribir a dinamot1981@gmail.com.
